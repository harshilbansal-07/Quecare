QueCareHospital queue

Know where you stand in the queue

QueCare gives each patient a token for their chosen doctor and shows how many patients are ahead. Patients are called in order, and reception keeps the queue fair.

In an emergency, do not wait for a token. Go straight to the emergency department or call 112.
